Privacy Policy – 2248 Koyaki

Last updated: November 6, 2026

This policy explains how the game 2248 Koyaki: Number Puzzle (package com.koyaki.koyaki2248), made by Koyaki Game, handles your information.

1. Data stored on your device

Your game progress (scores, best records, levels, stars, diamonds, Hammer and Shuffle items, player name, chosen avatar and settings) is saved only on your device. We do not ask for your email, phone number, location, photos or contacts. The country flag on the leaderboard comes from your device language setting and is not sent to us.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows banner ads, full-screen (pop-up) ads and optional reward ads from Google AdMob. To show ads, Google may collect your device advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interaction data. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or limit ad personalization, in your phone's Google settings. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. In-app purchases

Players can buy diamonds, a one-time "Remove Ads" purchase and a "Koyaki VIP" subscription. All payments are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. Subscriptions renew automatically and can be cancelled at any time in your Google Play subscriptions.

4. Children

This game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Parents can use Google Play parental controls to limit purchases.

5. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be shown on this page.

6. Contact

Privacy questions: koyakigame@gmail.com
